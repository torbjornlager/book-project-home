The Box Shop: a browser-only Web Prolog experiment.
Application source: https://github.com/torbjornlager/trinity-demonstrator/tree/6ed2a9f1106b48c2fdfa8315c051e06225996f50/experiments/boxshop

Browser speech recognition is required and may use an external browser service.
Orders are demonstration data only; nothing is purchased or stored.
